# Arcane Power asset workflow

Use the installed `blender-live-workflow` skill for Blender work. Work in the user's visible Blender window and inspect images after meaningful changes. Numerical checks and successful MWM builds do not establish visual quality.

- Use the actual live Blender viewport and fresh UI screenshots for improvement decisions. Verify the view has settled before judging it. After derived-model checks or exports, restore the finished main model and its useful review view; do not leave a distant LOD or export subpart active.

- Judge every changed joint, panel, fastener, pillar face and dispenser mount at three distances from the inspected surface: **1.8 m closest, then 2.4 m and 3.0 m**. Keep the target, focal length and lighting consistent across that set. Check front/side and chamber-facing views. Whole-assembly views and a 1.9 m engineer beside a 2.5 m grid cell supplement these detail checks; they do not replace them. Record the distances with the review evidence.
- Run `audit_energy_geometry.py` before native export; it checks authored solids, dimensions, glass seams, seating and fan clearances.
- Compare the Energy Core with the accepted main reactor at similar scale, lighting and camera angle. Reuse reactor rings, native SE detail assets and their UVs where suitable.
- Preserve the Energy Core's 3×3×3 large-grid size and upgrade modules' 1×3×1 size. Keep native SE upgrade slots unscaled. Version remains 0.1.1.
- Finish and inspect the main models first. Refine LODs and construction stages afterward, from the settled main geometry. Do not spend the main-model review loop polishing derived representations.
- Current work includes reactor exterior texture refinement, then updating stale construction stages and LODs from settled mains across the family. Energy Core construction stages already follow the new mains; retain them unless validation finds a mismatch. Reactor pillar proportions and upper roof seats are accepted; preserve them.
- Round forms are allowed wherever they suit the part, including cable sockets, connectors, pipes, cells, drums and fan grilles. Prefer hard edges and narrow chamfers on broad armor, housings, supports, panels and frames. Use restrained fillets where they improve a joint, handling edge or manufactured finish; avoid rounding every edge or softening the overall silhouette. Keep fillet radii modest for the part's scale and inspect their clearance at the required distances. Preserve native SE connector assets and their UVs.
- Full-height 1×3×1 modules use three unscaled native upgrade connectors at 2.5 m vertical spacing. Each core mating face must provide the matching three connectors. Seat surrounds and fasteners on real supports; avoid coplanar pad overlaps and oversized empty pockets.
- Service gaskets need solid backing around their full perimeter, including across armor seams. Keep structural fasteners clear of service panels, seals and dock roots; verify supported seating and edge clearance at arm’s length before export.
- Receiver lips must clear the casing face, and their lower shoulders must clear the full cover thickness. Inspect front, top and side receivers for coplanar overlap; preserve real louver-end support when widening apertures. Export validation uses evaluated triangulation, while export-only names, dummy display sizes and transforms are restored before further authoring.
- Keep pillar-foot flanges inside the actual supporting perimeter collar, including its polygon edges. Check the curved toe and corners, not only the centre. Status lamps need separate jacket, backing and emitter depths, with a real recess and unobstructed luminous face; do not stack coplanar surfaces.
- Prefer supported inset service doors and vents to broad gasket plates laid over armor seams. Give fasteners a clear panel or flange to secure; remove redundant rows buried in dock housings. Inspect matching top and bottom drum joints and check curved saddles against both the round skin and straight dock cheeks.
- Existing collision models are accepted. Preserve them unless a requested geometry change demonstrably requires an adjustment.
- For scripted motion, validate actual exported mesh vertices under the compiled runtime matrices across the full travel, including LODs. An identity subpart dummy alone does not establish that its mesh uses the main model's axes. Keep the native-motion contract sampled from the evaluated authoring rig.
- Diagnose texture complaints using normals, UV coverage and surface spacing. Separate intentional atlas reuse from coplanar geometry and z-fighting.
- Keep broad armor legible: use metric UV density and distinct per-panel origins, with heavy scratched paint limited to narrow edges. Reuse native decal UV rectangles, preserve their aspect ratio, and check both full backing and visibility against neighboring details. Transfer construction UVs by source face/corner when cleanup has changed loop order or triangulation.
- Use native SEUT materials, textures and detail assets as on the main reactor, preserving their UVs. Custom field/glass behavior is separate from structural surfaces. Native upgrade ports are open-backed assets: give each a closed receiver/backing and verify it from the chamber as well as the mating face.
- `build_energy_core.py` is procedural source. Reconcile accepted interactive edits into that source before regeneration. Use the existing SEUT/export pipeline and validate the resulting asset in SE when claiming game behavior.
- `Battery / assembled review` is a temporary inspection scene. Save it to a separate checkpoint; do not overwrite `arcane-energy-core.blend` with the review assembly.
- After main-mesh edits, refresh review copies with `sync_energy_review.py`. Activate and update each source and destination scene before reading world matrices; inactive-scene caches can be stale after reopening. Check the assembled and comparison scenes after reload, including each copy's fastener references.
- Keep Workshop gallery updates paused until the user lifts the hold. Do not push `main` as part of asset inspection: CI publishes the Workshop item.

## Cleanup after every modelling pass

Cleanup is a required final step after the requested work is saved and verified; do it without waiting for a separate user request.

- Put scratch scripts, logs, experimental exports and comparison renders in `ArcanePower/.cache/<task>/`. Keep temporary work out of `src`. Once a pass is complete, remove its disposable references, review captures/recordings, capture queues, rejected exports, one-off scripts, bytecode and inactive build intermediates. Keep evidence needed to diagnose an unresolved failure until it is resolved.
- Preserve production `.blend` files and named checkpoints, accepted collision inputs, native baselines, texture/icon sources, reusable builders/exporters/checks, current runtime assets and the current release ZIP. A script used to reproduce an accepted change is source, even if it was written during this pass. Preserve external recovery backups and unrelated user work. Ignored or untracked does not mean disposable.
- Audit script dependencies before removing obsolete authoring passes, including `runpy`, imported functions, export tools and manual workflow documentation. Move still-used helpers into their current owner before deleting a superseded entry point; thermal primitives live in `thermal_parts.py` and current viewport helpers in `review_thermal_models.py`. Remove orphaned inputs and stale documentation links with the retired script. A manual builder or audit is not unused merely because another script does not call it.
- Clean `validation/` too. Remove superseded reports, per-attempt dumps and review notes after recording useful conclusions in the existing project documentation. Keep the source contracts, fingerprints and manifests consumed by current tools, plus the latest delivery summary. Follow [validation retention rules](../validation/README.md) and the explicit exceptions in the repository `.gitignore`; inspect readers before deleting a new kind of report. Do not keep a growing series of cleanup reports.
- Before pruning export staging or old model revisions, preserve required rebuild inputs and verify the current runtime dependency closure. Use `tools/package_release.py` for its existing model-export pruning after a successful package build. Do not use blanket `git clean`, delete active process outputs, or discard unresolved export recovery data.
- Verify preserved files are unchanged, run the delivery check if packaged content or its supporting files were cleaned, and run `git diff --check`. Remove disposable output regenerated by those checks. Summarize what was removed and any retained dependencies; do not claim an in-game check unless one actually ran. Cleanup alone does not authorize a commit, push or Workshop publication.
